FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Common questions about our clinic, treatments, and payment options.

Contact us for more
Where is the clinic located exactly?

Our clinic is inside Ever More Medical Centre in Sabah Al-Salem – Kuwait. The location is convenient and easily accessible from many areas within Kuwait. If you like, message us on WhatsApp and we will help you with the best directions before your appointment.

What services do you provide at the clinic?

We offer comprehensive aesthetic and dermatology services including laser, skincare, fillers, Botox, glow sessions, and hair treatments. Each service has different options and protocols depending on your goal and skin/hair type. Therefore, it is best to determine what is suitable after the doctor's assessment rather than choosing randomly.

How is the suitable session determined?

The session is determined after the doctor assesses your condition and listens to the exact goal you want. Afterwards, they choose the most appropriate protocol based on your skin or hair type and the degree of need, explaining what to expect step by step. Based on the assessment, the number of sessions and the appropriate timing between them are determined if necessary.

Do I need to book before visiting the clinic?

Yes, advance booking is best to ensure a suitable time for you without waiting. Especially on busy days, booking helps us arrange the appointment according to the service and time that suits you. You can book from the website or via WhatsApp/call, and we will confirm your appointment before visiting.

How does booking from the website work?

You can choose the service from the website and make the booking directly in simple steps. If you are not sure what is most suitable, fill out the contact form and we will call you to confirm the appointment and arrange details. After confirming the appointment, you can continue with us on WhatsApp or call with complete ease.

When is payment made? And what payment methods are available?

Payment is made upon visiting inside the clinic after confirming the service details. Before starting the session, we clearly explain the cost and steps so everything is organized from the beginning. If you have an inquiry about a specific payment method, contact us and we will provide you with the appropriate details.

Is installment available? And what is the maximum number of installments?

Yes, installments are possible depending on the type of service and the plan determined by the doctor. The maximum number of installments is 4 payments, and the terms and installment method are clarified at the time of booking or upon visit. If you would like us to confirm installment availability for a specific service before your appointment, message us on WhatsApp.

Do sessions require recovery time?

It depends on the type of session and the nature of your skin/hair, and some sessions are light and do not require a long time. The doctor explains everything to you before the procedure: Is there slight redness? Are there specific instructions? And what to expect after the session. Based on the assessment, we arrange an appointment that suits your schedule and events.

Are there instructions before and after the session?

Yes, we give you clear instructions before and after the session to make the experience comfortable and the result better. Instructions vary depending on the type of service, such as skincare after laser or a simple routine after glow sessions. We answer any question you have regarding creams/routine/appropriate timing to return to daily activity.

Is there follow-up after the session?

Yes, follow-up depends on the type of session and the doctor's plan, especially if the sessions are part of a protocol. We give you post-session guidelines, and if you need a slight adjustment or have an inquiry about the result, we are with you step by step. Our goal is for everything to be clear and comfortable for you from the first session to the last step in the plan.
